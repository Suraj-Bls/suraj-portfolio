About

Engineering systems that other teams rely on.

I'm a Senior Software Developer at Nomura Securities International in New York, working where large-scale infrastructure meets applied AI.

At Nomura I led the migration of 10 critical applications off end-of-support TIBCO messaging onto Solace and Apache Pulsar in six months. That included 9 core data flows and a regulatory system, with zero production disruption, and it contributed to the firm's DORA compliance. I also built a RAG-based assistant that gives developers and clients 24/7 first-line support, and an MCP agent framework for natural-language access to reference data that 20+ global teams now use, across Quants, Risk, Finance, Compliance, Legal, Research and Technology.

Before Nomura I built asynchronous metrics pipelines on AWS for MySQL HeatWave at Oracle, and spent two years at JPMorgan Chase delivering Kafka-based client-onboarding systems. That work earned back-to-back firm-level Execution Excellence Awards. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University at Buffalo and am lead author of three research papers published by IEEE and Springer.

I care about clean architecture, careful rollouts and tools that make the people around me faster.
